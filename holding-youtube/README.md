# Holding YouTube

A private, single-user tool that uploads and schedules videos for the owner's own YouTube channel
(2D Satisfying Simulations). It is used only by the channel owner; it has no other users.

What it does, with the owner's permission (Google OAuth):

- uploads finished videos as private previews and sets their thumbnails,
- schedules a video for publication after the owner has watched and approved it,
- posts one comment from the channel after publication,
- reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports.
